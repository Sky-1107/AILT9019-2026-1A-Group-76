LP Lesson-Plan Scaffolder For tutors · not student-facing

Tutor workspace

Draft a credible lesson plan in minutes.

Turn a short teaching brief into a structured first draft: learning objectives, a time allocation, an activity sequence, and tutor check-questions — editable before you teach. This is a planning aid that supports, not replaces, your judgment.

Support tool, not a script: follow the draft only where it serves your class. Every draft is preliminary and non-accredited; tutor review is required. The buffer feature exists so rigid pacing does not crowd out learning.

This tool does not

  1. Access, grade, or store data or rosters of real students.
  2. Accept official student coursework or personally identifiable information (PII).
  3. Claim or certify that a draft is an officially accredited curriculum.
